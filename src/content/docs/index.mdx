			<span class="ws-row-num">01</span>
			<h3>Efficient AI</h3>
			<p>What actually happens when a model runs on hardware, from a single GPU to a large cluster? I'm curious how memory, model design, and communication shape training, and what it takes to make it faster and more efficient.</p>
		</li>
		<li>
			<span class="ws-row-num">02</span>
			<h3>Large language models</h3>
			<p>How does a model learn from sentences, reason with them, and pick up language along the way? I'm also curious how that ability extends into new directions, such as agentic LLMs that plan and act.</p>
		</li>
		<li>
			<span class="ws-row-num">03</span>
			<h3>Physical AI</h3>
			<p>What can AI models do in the real world once they are paired with robots and other physical systems? It's a newer interest of mine, and one I'm eager to explore further.</p>
		</li>
	</ol>
